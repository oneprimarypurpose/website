Finances

Treasurer

Cares for the group's funds so it can keep carrying the message.

Requirements

  • Suggested minimum clean time: 6 months — this position handles money and needs stability and trust.
  • Organized and reliable with simple record-keeping.
  • Plus the general requirements for every trusted servant.

Responsibilities

  • Count and safeguard the Seventh Tradition contributions — the in-person basket and Venmo (@Steve-Melton-8).
  • Pay the group's expenses, such as rent and coffee.
  • Maintain the prudent reserve of $200.
  • Give the Treasury report at the Business Meeting.
  • Carry out group-conscience donations to the next level of service.

Term

A one-year commitment, renewable by group conscience, with attendance at the monthly Business Meeting.

Zoom Host

Runs the meeting on Zoom and holds the host controls and security settings.

Online · security   

Group Guidelines Secretaries Zoom Host Zoom Co-Host GSR Alt GSR Treasurer Business Mtg. Chair

Role

The Zoom Host runs the online side of the hybrid meeting. They start the meeting from the account that owns it, hold the host controls, and are responsible for the security settings that keep the meeting safe from disruption.

Requirements

  • Willingness and desire to serve
  • Actively working the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of Narcotics Anonymous
  • Active participation in the group
  • A one-year commitment
  • Attends the monthly Business Meeting

Duties

  • Start the meeting about 15 minutes early from the account that owns it, so they hold the host controls.
  • Confirm the security settings: Waiting Room on; participants join muted with video off; screen sharing limited to host & co-host.
  • Make the Zoom Co-Host a co-host as soon as they join.
  • Admit known members from the Waiting Room.
  • Post the Venmo (Seventh Tradition) and meeting-confirmation messages in the chat.
  • Spotlight the speaker and make sure the room audio carries to Zoom.
  • Protect the meeting from disruption — stop video, mute, or remove as needed.
  • Send meeting confirmations after the meeting.

Step-by-step operational detail — with screenshots — lives in the Zoom Co-Host Setup resource.

If the meeting is disrupted

Security response

Stop video / mute / remove the disruptor. If there are several, use Security → Suspend Participant Activities to freeze everything and lock the meeting. If it won't stop, end and restart with new credentials shared only with known members.

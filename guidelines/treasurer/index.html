Treasurer

Cares for the group's funds so it can keep carrying the message.

Finances

Role

The Treasurer manages the group's funds. Prudent, responsible management of those funds safeguards the group's ability to carry the message of recovery — all financial resources are dedicated to that purpose.

Requirements

  • Willingness and desire to serve
  • Actively working the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of Narcotics Anonymous
  • Active participation in the group
  • A minimum of 2 years clean
  • Has a bank account
  • A one-year commitment
  • Attends the monthly Business Meeting

Duties

  • Count and safeguard the Seventh Tradition contributions — the in-person basket and Venmo (@Steve-Melton-8).
  • Pay the group's expenses, such as rent and coffee.
  • Maintain the prudent reserve of $200.
  • Give the Treasury report at the monthly Business Meeting.
  • Carry out group-conscience donations to the next level of service.

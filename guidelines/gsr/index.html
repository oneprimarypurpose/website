Role

The Group Service Representative (GSR) links the group to the rest of Narcotics Anonymous. They represent the group's conscience at the Greater San Jose Area Service Committee and bring Area news back to the group.

Requirements

  • Willingness and desire to serve
  • Actively working the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of Narcotics Anonymous
  • Active participation in the group
  • A minimum of 1 year clean
  • A one-year commitment
  • Attends the monthly Business Meeting

Duties

  • Attend the Area Service Committee meetings each month.
  • Represent the group's conscience and vote on its behalf.
  • Give the GSR report at the monthly Business Meeting.
  • Work with the Alternate GSR so they are ready to step in.

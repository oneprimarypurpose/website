Business Meeting Chair

Leads the monthly Business Meeting and keeps the group's records.

Governance   

Group Guidelines Secretaries Zoom Host Zoom Co-Host GSR Alt GSR Treasurer Business Mtg. Chair

Role

The Business Meeting Chair leads the monthly Business Meeting and keeps the group's records, so the group's decisions are made by conscience and properly documented.

Requirements

  • Willingness and desire to serve
  • Actively working the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of Narcotics Anonymous
  • Active participation in the group
  • A minimum of 6 months clean
  • A one-year commitment
  • Attends the monthly Business Meeting

Duties

  • Calls the Business Meeting to order and leads it.
  • Follows the Business Meeting format.
  • Maintains the monthly meeting records.
  • Takes the minutes and emails them to the group.
  • Serves as tie-breaker in a vote.

The Business Meeting

Held on the second Sunday of each month, immediately following the regular meeting. All are welcome to attend; all attendees have a voice and a vote.

Agenda

  1. Open the meeting — Serenity Prayer; take attendance.
  2. Minutes — any additions or changes; approval of the previous minutes.
  3. Reports — GSR report; Treasurer report.
  4. Old Business
  5. New Business
  6. Open Forum
  7. Closing prayer

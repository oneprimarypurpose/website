Three-person rotation

Secretaries

Three trusted servants who take turns chairing the weekly meeting, so the responsibility is shared and every Sunday is covered.

Requirements

  • Suggested minimum clean time: 6 months.
  • Comfortable leading a room and reading the meeting format aloud.
  • Plus the general requirements for every trusted servant.

Responsibilities

  • Open and lead the meeting using the group's approved format (the secretary script).
  • Arrive by 9:45 AM to set up before the meeting.
  • Welcome newcomers and visitors warmly.
  • Keep the meeting on time and on format.
  • Coordinate the rotation so every Sunday is covered.
  • Hand off cleanly to the next secretary in the rotation.

Term

A one-year commitment in the three-person rotation, renewable by group conscience, with attendance at the monthly Business Meeting. The term starts January 1, with elections held in December.

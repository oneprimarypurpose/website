  • A suggested minimum of [ clean time — e.g., 1 year ] for lead and chairing positions
  • A [ commitment length — e.g., 1 year ] commitment, with attendance at the monthly Business Meeting
Three-person rotation

Secretaries

Three trusted servants who take turns chairing the weekly meeting, so the responsibility is shared and every Sunday is covered.

Duties

  • Open and lead the meeting using the group's approved format (the secretary script).
  • Welcome newcomers and visitors warmly.
  • Keep the meeting on time and on format.
  • Coordinate the rotation so every Sunday is covered.
  • Hand off cleanly to the next secretary in the rotation.

The full secretary format — readings, announcements, Seventh Tradition, newcomer and chip-meeting language, speaker introduction, and the closing — is the Meeting Script. Read it through before you chair.

Online · security

Zoom Host

Starts the meeting, holds the host controls, and keeps the meeting running smoothly and safe from disruption.

Before the meeting (~15 minutes early)

  1. Sign in to Zoom on the account that owns the meeting [ Steve / Roger ] and start it from there — the only way you hold host controls.
  2. Open Security and confirm Waiting Room is on.
  3. Confirm participants join muted and with video off by default.
  4. Set Share Screen to Host & Co-Host only; leave Rename Themselves off.
  5. When the Co-Host joins, hover their tile → More → Make Co-Host.

During the meeting

  • Admit known members from the Waiting Room (let the Co-Host handle most of this).
  • Post the Venmo and meeting-confirmation messages in the chat.
  • Spotlight the speaker; make sure the room audio carries to Zoom.
  • Watch for disruption — stop video, mute, or remove as needed.

After the meeting

  • Send meeting confirmations to anyone who privately messaged their email.
If the meeting is disrupted

Stop video / mute / remove the disruptor. If there are several, use Security → Suspend Participant Activities to freeze everything and lock the meeting. If it won't stop, end and restart with new credentials shared only with known members.

In person · second computer

Zoom Co-Host

The meeting's second set of eyes — the security partner who runs the Zoom side from the second computer in the room.

Avoid audio feedback

The host's computer carries the room audio. Keep this second computer's mic and speaker off (or wear headphones), or the two computers will echo. You manage by watching the screen.

Before the meeting

  1. Arrive in person and sit at the second computer.
  2. Join the Zoom meeting, then ask the Host to Make you Co-Host.
  3. Mute your mic and turn your speaker off (or use headphones) before anything else — the #1 cause of feedback.

During the meeting

  • Vet and admit people from the Waiting Room; hold anyone with a blank, random, or odd name.
  • Watch the participant list — stop video, mute, or remove disruptors, and alert the host.
  • Keep an eye on the chat.
  • Stay in the background — protect the meeting, don't co-pilot it. Keep names and emails confidential.
Area service

GSR & Alternate GSR

The Group Service Representative carries the group's voice to Area and brings information back.

Duties

  • Attend the Area service committee meetings.
  • Represent the group's conscience and vote on its behalf.
  • Report Area news back to the group at the Business Meeting (the GSR report).
  • The Alternate GSR backs up the GSR and steps in when needed.
Finances

Treasurer

Cares for the group's funds so it can keep carrying the message.

Duties

  • Count and safeguard the Seventh Tradition contributions — the in-person basket and Venmo (@Steve-Melton-8).
  • Pay the group's expenses, such as rent and coffee.
  • Maintain the prudent reserve of [ $ ____ ].
  • Give the Treasury report at the Business Meeting.
  • Carry out group-conscience donations to the next level of service.
Governance

Business Meeting Chair

Leads the monthly Business Meeting and keeps the group's records. May be a Secretary or a separate position — group to decide.

Duties

  • Calls the Business Meeting to order and leads it.
  • Follows the Business Meeting format.
  • Maintains the monthly meeting records.
  • Takes the minutes and emails them to the group.
  • Serves as tie-breaker in a vote.

The Business Meeting

Held the second Sunday of each month, immediately after the regular meeting. All are welcome; all attendees have a voice and a vote.

  1. Open — Serenity Prayer; take attendance.
  2. Minutes — additions or changes; approve the previous minutes.
  3. Reports — GSR report; Treasurer report.
  4. Old Business
  5. New Business
  6. Open Forum
  7. Closing prayer

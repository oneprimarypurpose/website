Governance

Business Meeting Chair

Leads the monthly Business Meeting and keeps the group's records.

May be a Secretary or a separate position — group to decide.

Requirements

  • Suggested minimum clean time: 6 months.
  • Comfortable running a meeting and keeping written minutes.
  • Plus the general requirements for every trusted servant.

Responsibilities

  • Calls the Business Meeting to order and leads it.
  • Follows the Business Meeting format.
  • Maintains the monthly meeting records.
  • Takes the minutes and distributes them to the group by email or text.
  • Serves as tie-breaker in a vote.

Term

A one-year commitment, renewable by group conscience.

The Business Meeting

Held the second Sunday of each month, immediately after the regular meeting. All are welcome; all attendees have a voice and a vote.

  1. Open — Serenity Prayer; take attendance.
  2. Minutes — additions or changes; approve the previous minutes.
  3. Reports — GSR report; Treasurer report.
  4. Old Business
  5. New Business
  6. Open Forum
  7. Closing prayer

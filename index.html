New here?

You don't have to share, say anything, or have it figured out. Just come.

Whether it's your first 30 days, your first meeting ever, or you're visiting from out of the area, you're welcome here. We'll make a point of saying hello.

What's said here stays here. Anonymity is the spiritual foundation of our program, and it's what makes it safe to be honest. You belong in the room exactly as you are today.

We open with a moment of silence and the Serenity Prayer, and close with the Third Step Prayer. In between, one addict talks to another, the only requirement for membership is a desire to stop using.

How the group works

Our purpose

We follow the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of NA. Everything we do is in service of carrying the message of recovery.

Seventh Tradition

We're fully self-supporting through our own contributions. In the room there's a basket; on Zoom you can give via Venmo at @Steve-Melton-8, posted in the chat.

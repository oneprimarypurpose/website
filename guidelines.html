Narcotics Anonymous

Group Guidelines

How the group runs, what it stands for, and how decisions get made, so we can keep carrying the message.

Draft for group conscience

This is a working draft. The items in gold are choices for the group to make and fill in before adopting. Bring it to a Business Meeting for group conscience.

Purpose and Function

These guidelines were developed to help the group carry the NA message of recovery more effectively. They reflect the experience, strength, and hope of this group. All members are encouraged to read them and abide by them so the group can continue to carry the message.

This group adheres to the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of Narcotics Anonymous.

For anything not addressed in these guidelines, the group follows the [ local Area Guide to Local Services — confirm name ].

Format

The group meets Sundays at 10:00 AM Pacific as a one-hour speaker and discussion meeting in a hybrid format — members attend both in person and on Zoom.

This is a closed NA meeting, open to those who are addicts or think they may have a drug problem.

  • Chip meeting is held on the last Sunday of each month.
  • On other Sundays, recovery birthdays from the previous 30 days are acknowledged.

The secretaries follow the group's approved meeting format. That format was developed by group conscience, and it is strongly suggested not to deviate from it.

Financial Operations

Prudent, responsible management of the group's funds safeguards its ability to carry the message of recovery. All financial resources are dedicated to that purpose.

Prudent Reserve

The group keeps a prudent reserve of $ ____ to cover its regular expenses, such as rent and coffee.

Seventh Tradition

The Seventh Tradition is the group's sole source of funds. In-person members contribute to the basket; Zoom members may contribute via Venmo at @Steve-Melton-8, posted in the chat.

After the group's expenses are paid and the prudent reserve is maintained, the group decides by conscience at the monthly Business Meeting how much, if any, to donate to the next level of service (for example, Area).

Business Meeting Procedures

The Business Meeting is held on the second Sunday of each month, immediately following the regular meeting. All are welcome to attend; all attendees have a voice and a vote.

Agenda

  1. Open the meeting — Serenity Prayer; take attendance.
  2. Minutes — any additions or changes; approval of the previous minutes.
  3. Reports — GSR report; Treasurer report.
  4. Old Business
  5. New Business
  6. Open Forum
  7. Closing prayer

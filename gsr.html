Area service

GSR & Alternate GSR

The Group Service Representative carries the group's voice to Area and brings information back.

Requirements

  • GSR — suggested minimum clean time: 1 year.
  • Alternate GSR — suggested minimum clean time: 6 months, with a willingness to act as GSR when needed and a willingness to take on the full GSR position afterward.
  • Able to attend Area service committee meetings [ when / where ].
  • Plus the general requirements for every trusted servant.

Responsibilities

  • Attend the Area service committee meetings.
  • Represent the group's conscience and vote on its behalf.
  • Report Area news back to the group at the Business Meeting (the GSR report).
  • The Alternate GSR backs up the GSR, attends when the GSR cannot, and steps in when needed — with the willingness to act as GSR and to take on the full position afterward.

Term

A one-year commitment, with the Alternate serving alongside and stepping up to the full GSR position afterward.

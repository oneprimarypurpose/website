Zoom Host — step by step

The day-of guide for whoever is hosting on Zoom. Follow it from the top — you don't need to know Zoom already. Tick each step as you go; your progress is saved on this device.

Requirements

  • Comfortable running Zoom, and trusted with the host account and security settings.
  • Plus the general requirements for every trusted servant.

Phase 1 · Sign in & start the meeting

  1. Sign in to Zoom with the group's host account. On a computer, go to zoom.us and click Sign In (top right) — or open the Zoom app and click Sign In.
    • Email: [ host account email ]
    • Password: kept private — it's shared with hosts directly. Ask [ who to ask ] if you don't have it. Never write it on this page or in the chat.
    • Tip: check "Stay signed in" so you don't have to do this every week.
  2. Start the meeting. Click Meetings in the left menu, find One Primary Purpose, and click Start.
    • Don't see it? Click Join and enter Meeting ID 139 842 662, passcode 101293.
    • When asked, choose Join with Computer Audio.
  3. Make your Co-Host. When the Co-Host joins, open Participants, hover their name, and click More → Make Co-Host. Now two of you can watch the room.

Phase 2 · Lock it down (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open the Security menu (the shield icon). At the bottom of the window, click the Security shield and make sure these are set:
    • Enable Waiting Room — checked.
    • Share Screen — leave this so only Host & Co-Host can share.
    • Under "Allow participants to," keep Unmute themselves checked so people can share when called on.
  2. Have people enter quietly. In Participants, click ··· (More) at the bottom and turn on Mute Participants on Entry. The Co-Host keeps an eye on video as people come in.

Phase 3 · As people arrive

  1. Admit people from the Waiting Room. Admit anyone you recognize by name. Hold anyone with a blank, random, or odd name. At the start you can admit in small batches; once it settles, admit one at a time.
  2. Post these two messages in the chat. Open Chat and paste:
    7th Tradition — Zoom donations by Venmo: @Steve-Melton-8 (or scan the QR on our welcome page). Thank you for supporting the meeting.
    For a meeting confirmation, please message host [ name ] your email and you'll receive one after the meeting.

Phase 4 · During the meeting

  • Spotlight the speaker. When the speaker begins, hover their video (or the in-room podium camera) and click More → Spotlight for Everyone, so online members focus on them. Check that the room mic is picking up their voice.
  • Keep half an eye on the room. Watch the Participants list and videos. If a video turns on that shouldn't be there, stop it. Otherwise, enjoy the meeting.
If someone disrupts the meeting

Stay calm and don't engage them. Hover their name → More → Stop Video, Mute, or Remove (keep "don't allow rejoin" on). If there's more than one, click Security → Suspend Participant Activities — this instantly freezes all video, audio, and screen share and locks the meeting; then re-admit your members. If it won't stop, End Meeting for All and restart with a new Meeting ID, sharing the new link only with members.

Phase 5 · After the meeting

  • Send confirmations, then end. Email a confirmation to anyone who asked, then click End → End Meeting for All. That's it — you're done.

## Introducing the speaker

*(Zoom reminder, before introducing the speaker)*

1. Please be mindful that this is a hybrid meeting. To be respectful to everyone attending via Zoom, please stand here at the podium, on camera, so those on Zoom can hear your message of recovery.

**Introduce the speaker:**

2. I'd like to introduce our speaker, who will share their experience, strength, and hope for the next **15 to 20 minutes**, and then call on people as time allows.
3. If called upon, please limit your share to **3–5 minutes**, allowing as many people as possible to share.
4. We do have a timer — if you see it flash, please acknowledge it and wrap up. Thank you.

*After the speaker shares, thank them and return the meeting back to the speaker.*

5. The speaker may choose a topic or simply call on people, as they wish.

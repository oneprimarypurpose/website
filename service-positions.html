General requirements — every trusted servant

These apply to every position. Each position's own page lists what it adds on top.

  • Actively working the Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions of Narcotics Anonymous
  • Active participation in the group
  • Attendance at the monthly Business Meeting for the length of the commitment

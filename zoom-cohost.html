In person · second computer

Zoom Co-Host

The meeting's second set of eyes — the security partner who runs the Zoom side from the second computer in the room.

Requirements

  • Suggested minimum clean time: [ e.g., 90 days ].
  • Able to attend in person at the meeting most Sundays.
  • Plus the general requirements for every trusted servant.

Where you serve from

The Co-Host role could be done remotely, but we ask you to come in person and run the Zoom side from the second computer in the room — so you can talk to the host directly and react instantly.

Avoid audio feedback

The host's computer carries the room audio. Keep this second computer's mic and speaker off (or wear headphones), or the two computers will echo. You manage by watching the screen.

Before the meeting

  1. Arrive 10–15 minutes early and settle at the second computer.
  2. Join the meeting and ask the Host to Make you Co-Host.
  3. Mute your mic and turn your speaker off (or use headphones) before anything else — the #1 cause of feedback.
  4. Open the Participants panel, the Waiting Room, and the Chat, and keep them visible.

During the meeting

  • Vet and admit known members from the Waiting Room; hold anyone with a blank, random, or odd name.
  • Watch the participant list — stop video, mute, or remove disruptors, and alert the host.
  • Keep an eye on the chat for spam or links from unknown names.
  • You'll see members' names and emails. Keep them confidential — anonymity is the foundation of the program.
  • Stay in the background — protect the meeting, don't co-pilot it.

Term

A [ e.g., 6-month ] commitment, renewable by group conscience, with attendance at the monthly Business Meeting.
